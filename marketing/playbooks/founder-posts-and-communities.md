# Write founder posts that start conversations, and earn a place in communities
**Use when:** posts get impressions but no leads, a community post got removed or ignored, you're going back to posting after a gap, or you want to use Reddit, forums, LinkedIn or launch sites without spamming.  ·  **Sources:** Breakthrough Advertising (Schwartz), Contagious (Berger), Made to Stick (Heath & Heath), This Is Marketing (Godin), Traction (Weinberg & Mares); also Building a StoryBrand (Miller), Influence (Cialdini), How Brands Grow (Sharp), The Lean Startup (Ries)

## Method
1. **Earn standing before you post** [Godin] [Cialdini] [Weinberg]. Pick two or three communities where your buyers already talk. Spend a few weeks answering threads usefully, about twenty answers per community, without linking to yourself. Takers get ignored or removed. People who gave first get read.
2. **Open with the reader's problem, not your news** [Schwartz]. Line one names something they already feel, in their words. "We launched" and "I'm back" only interest people who already want you.
3. **Tie it to a trigger** [Berger]. Attach the problem to something already on their mind this week: an industry change, a public event, a common moment in their job. Check every fact in the hook. Your readers will [Field].
4. **Make the insight impossible to drop** [Berger] [Heath]. Build the story so it can't be retold without your point. Surprise should land on the core message, not on a gimmick. The best proof is testable or personal: you tried it on yourself, here's what happened.
5. **Cast yourself as the guide** [Miller]. Your own failure or struggle is backstory that earns empathy and authority. The post ends on the reader's situation and what they could do, not on your product.
6. **Ask a real question instead of a call to action** [Godin]. A discussion question invites peers in. "Comment X for the link" asks for permission you haven't earned yet. Disclose that you're building something in the area. Share the link only when someone asks, or in the first comment where the rules allow.
7. **Write for one peer group** [Godin]. "People like us" is the strongest pull. Post where you're a genuine member, in that group's language.
8. **Stay on air** [Sharp]. A steady rhythm beats a burst after silence. Never announce the gap.
9. **Score by conversations** [Ries] [Weinberg]. Count replies from buyers, DMs that turn into calls and qualified leads per post. Impressions and reactions are vanity. Give each channel a fixed trial (about a month) and drop it if it produced no qualified conversation.

## Rules of thumb
- Reply to every comment in the first hour with a real sentence. Comments are where the conversations start.
- Comment on others' posts more than you post, adding a detail or a respectful disagreement.
- A post that taught something gets shared. A pitch rarely does [Berger].
- One idea per post. Cut everything that isn't the point.
- [Field] New or low-karma accounts are often auto-filtered, and some sites lock posting until you've commented. Check the rules and any "search first" policy before posting, and never repost removed text.
- [Field] Launch sites and directories bring backlinks and a few makers, rarely buyers. They're cheap experiments, not a launch (`/gtm` launch-strategy).

## Mistakes
- Pitching in the first line, or in a community you joined that day.
- A wrong fact in a news hook.
- Making yourself the hero of the story.
- Running six platforms for a week each instead of three for a month.
- Calling reach "traction".

## Template
```
POST: <community>, <date>
Standing earned here: __ useful answers so far (target ~20)
Reader's problem, in their words (line 1): ____________
Trigger this week: ____________ (fact-checked: source ____ )
My proof (personal/testable): ____________
The point a reader would retell: ____________
Ending question: ____________
Disclosure line: ____________   Link: only on request | first comment
SCORE after 48 h: buyer replies __ | DMs __ | calls booked __ | qualified leads __
```

## Validate it
- **Assumption created:** "Buyers in community C engage with problem story P" (`A#`, `[ASSUMPTION, E0]`).
- **Cheapest test:** the same story in two communities where you've earned standing, scored on buyer conversations after 48 hours.
- **Evidence bar:** impressions and reactions E0 · a buyer's substantive comment E1–E2 · a DM or call booked E3 · a paid conversion E4. Keep the channel only with E3 from ≥3 independent buyers inside its trial window.
