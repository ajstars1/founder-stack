# Decide who to sell to and build the list
**Use when:** you don't know which accounts to chase, the list is "everyone", or outreach converts unevenly.  ·  **Sources:** Founding Sales (Kazanjy), Predictable Revenue (Ross), Gap Selling (Keenan), The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick)

## Method
1. **Start from problems, not firmographics.** List each problem you solve with its impact and root cause. Then ask which industries, sizes, departments and roles suffer each one most [Keenan].
2. **Write a one-page ICP** [Ross]:
   - 3–5 traits you can check from outside;
   - red flags and deal-breakers (e.g. just signed a competitor, no owner for the problem, a category brand new to them);
   - the ideal contact, by role and situation;
   - the challenges that contact names.
   With customers, model it on your best 5–10%. Without them, model it on earlyvangelist traits [Fitzpatrick].
3. **Add demand signals.** These are observable hints of pain or urgency: job posts for work you automate, a funding round, a new regulation, a tool in their stack, rapid headcount growth. Add size-of-prize signals (seats, sites, volume), then combine both into one attractiveness score [Kazanjy].
4. **Target the middle.** Tiny accounts pay little and churn; huge ones are slow and entrenched. Your first wins sit between [Kazanjy].
5. **Slice until findable.** For each slice, name who wants it most, why, and where they gather. If you can't say where to find them, slice again [Fitzpatrick].
6. **Build the list from one good data source**, 50–100 accounts to start. Find a cascade of contacts per account: the likely owner, their boss, a peer. Note any warm path [Kazanjy]. Use `/secondary-research` for sources and current data.
7. **Rank by pain.** A = many problems with big impact. B = fewer problems, still big impact. C = many small problems. D = drop [Keenan].
8. **Track account status, not just leads:** cold, working, active opportunity, check back later, dead, customer, bad fit. In "working", the goal is the truth about fit, not an opportunity at any cost [Ross].
9. **Revise monthly** from closed-won and closed-lost reasons. Keep at most a handful of profiles [Ross].

## Rules of thumb
- Qualify the account first, then the person [Kazanjy].
- Go deep, not wide. Ten accounts worked ten times each beat a hundred touched once [Ross].
- Most teams aim too low in the org. Start at the level that owns the outcome and get referred down [Ross].
- An ICP that fits more than a few profiles is a marketing focus problem, not a sales list [Ross].
- An ICP built from demographics alone predicts nothing. Every trait should be a reason the pain exists or is affordable.
- Outbound with SDRs pays when a customer is worth roughly $5–10k+ a year [Ross]. Below that, lean on `/marketing` and product-led channels.
- Don't sell to stated need alone. Buyers misread their own problems, so the signal is pain plus impact [Keenan].

## Mistakes
- Picking accounts by who you know.
- Buying a giant generic list and blasting it [Kazanjy].
- Treating every inbound signup as an equal lead.
- Writing an ICP once and never updating it from wins and losses.
- Chasing whale logos that will take 18 months and a security review.

```
  1. ________  2. ________  3. ________

  - ________ → ________
Size-of-prize signals: ________
Red flags / deal-breakers: ________

Their likely top challenges: ________ [A#, E_]

ACCOUNT SHEET
| Account | Must-haves met | Signals | Size | Pain rank A–D | Contacts (cascade) | Warm path | Status |

Won deals matched ICP? __/__   Lost to bad fit? __   Trait to add/drop: ____
```

## Validate it
- **Assumptions created:** "accounts with signal X convert better than those without" and "role R owns the problem" (`A#`, `[ASSUMPTION, E0]`).
- **Cheapest test:** split the first 100 accounts by one signal, run the identical sequence, and compare reply → meeting → qualified-opportunity rates.
- **Evidence bar:** a positive reply is E2 (observed behaviour). A held meeting with the ICP contact is E3. The ICP is working-validated when ≥3 closed-won customers (E4) match it, and when your losses cluster outside it. Firmographic facts you look up are F# with a source. What you guess about their pain stays A# until conversations grade it.
- Small samples are directions, not verdicts. Re-run when the segment changes. Segment choice itself goes to `/where-to-play`.
