# Curriculum

Each path runs from foundations to advanced topics. Each entry is a playbook, and each playbook's
**Method** steps are the individual concepts: teach one step or rule per lesson. Read only the path
being studied.

## Discovery: find a problem worth solving
1. facts-assumptions-unknowns: the ledger and the E0–E4 evidence grades
2. where-to-play: choose a market from your abilities
3. problem-discovery: who suffers, and what their world looks like
4. customer-discovery: interviews that produce evidence
5. sales/playbooks/customer-conversations-mom-test: questions that can't be lied to
6. jtbd: the job, not the product category
7. value-proposition-canvas: fit between jobs/pains/gains and the offer
8. experiments: test cards, pass/fail bars, price tests
9. business-model-canvas: how it makes money; Get/Keep/Grow
10. startup-scorecard: score the venture on evidence
